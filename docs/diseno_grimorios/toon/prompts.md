## Prompt común

> Use the attached concept of the closed grimoire as the material, color and rendering reference. Show that exact book OPEN, viewed strictly top-down, as a cohesive fantasy game UI texture. Preserve its distinctive colors and chunky modeled shape. Match a stylized hand-painted 3D game prop with strong rich pigment, deep colored contact shadows under the page stack, confident color blocks and small crisp highlights on beveled surfaces. The shadow tones are clearly darker than the midtones. Pages are honey ochre at the edges and warm tan at the writing centers, with subtle hand-painted tonal variation, low contrast only inside writing fields. No pastel colors, no flat vector icon look, no photorealism, no thin ornate engraving. The open book fills the landscape canvas on a deep warm charcoal background; centered straight vertical spine, ample clean page surfaces, simple small corner ornaments well outside the writing fields, no quill.
> Use a precise consistent layout. LEFT PAGE top: a compact magical result circle centered at x=28%, y=30% of full spread. Its corona has exactly six empty small round glyph sockets at 12, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 o'clock. These six small sockets are the only elements around the rim. LEFT PAGE bottom: two very clear large empty writing circles side-by-side, centered around y=72%. Seals on the left, glyphs on the right; the right circle is 20% larger in diameter. Clean paper in both. RIGHT PAGE: an understated reference index of eight empty circular cells in two rows of four at the top and nine empty softly squared cells in three rows of three at the bottom. All functional marks rendered in dark pigmented ink with a subtle warm highlight, empty centers, comfortable clear space between groups. No visible text or letters.
