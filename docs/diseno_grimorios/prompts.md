# Láminas de los tres grimorios

Propuestas de arte generadas con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Los huecos dibujados sirven de referencia para encajar después el arte con las coordenadas de Godot.

## Prompt común

> Game UI art, stylized-concept, an open two-page interior spread of one fantasy grimoire viewed perfectly straight overhead, orthographic flat lay. Match a shared series art direction: 1470:1070 landscape composition, center vertical spine at 50%, book fills about 95% of width and 82% of height with all corners visible, both page faces the same size, dark charcoal neutral field, tactile painted storybook material and crisp restrained ink markings, readable at game scale. Keep warm ivory low-contrast writing fields, all designs wholly inside the paper, soft even lighting, leave a little open header space for runtime interface, no text of any kind, no letters, no numbers, no hands, no UI chrome, no objects outside the book. Render one high quality spread.

## Botánico

> Botanical apprentice grimoire. Warm tawny leather visible at edges, olive sage watercolor bramble and restrained botanical illuminated corners, aged ivory parchment, small brass furniture. Left page: one large thin circular drawing diagram at around 30% of spread width and 45% height, one clear smaller seal circle in its upper half, three unobtrusive smaller glyph sockets along its lower arc with blank interiors. Right page: compact reference area with a row of six small round empty cells and below nine subtle rounded cells in a 3 by 3 grid. Keep centers open and clean.

## Rúnico

> Runic grimoire for an experienced practitioner. Deep burgundy-brown leather, red ochre and antique brass, restrained angular chiseled knots and geometry in the corners, parchment slightly cream. Left page: one large thin circular working diagram in its center with one blank seal medallion and three blank glyph sockets around its lower arc, compass-like fine etched tick marks outside only. Right page: six small round seal cells in one row and nine blank subtle angular glyph cells in a 3 by 3 grid. Keep marks sparse and disciplined.

## Legendario

> Legendary advanced grimoire. Midnight blue binding with aged brass, subtle indigo and muted gold marginal illumination, celestial navigation motifs, exceptionally restrained ornament. Left page must have TWO distinct tiers with clear spacing: in the upper half ONE large magical result circle containing exactly two blank central medallions side by side and six small satellite sockets. In the lower half exactly TWO separate large EMPTY circles side by side, left circle for drawing seals and right circle for drawing glyphs; the two large bottom circles are equal size with generous clean interiors, neither circle touches the fold, margins, or upper diagram. Right page: four round seal cells, a second row of four round seal cells, and a distinct lower group of nine blank glyph cells arranged 4 and 5. No extra writing circle.
